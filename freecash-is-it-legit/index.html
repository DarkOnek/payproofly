Is Freecash Legit?

Freecash is a rewards platform where users can earn by completing surveys, playing games, testing apps and completing other offers. Here's what we found about how it works, payments, bonuses and the important things to know before signing up.

Last reviewed: September 28, 2026

Is Freecash legit? Quick answer

Freecash is a real rewards platform operated by Almedia, but users should understand its rules, eligibility requirements and withdrawal conditions before using it.

Freecash allows users to earn rewards by completing activities such as surveys, games, app offers and other tasks. The platform says it has paid out hundreds of millions of dollars in rewards, while its current terms identify Almedia GmbH as the operator of the service.

There was also significant scrutiny of Freecash in 2026 after reports concerning its marketing and mobile apps. The company disputed the allegations, and Freecash later announced that its app had returned to both the Apple App Store and Google Play.

What is Freecash?

Freecash is a get-paid-to, or GPT, platform. Instead of paying users simply for visiting a website, it offers rewards for completing specific activities provided through the platform and its partners.

These activities can include answering surveys, playing games, testing apps, completing offers and other tasks. The available opportunities depend on factors such as location, account eligibility and current campaigns.

Freecash's current terms state that users must be at least 16 years old and that only one account is permitted per person and per household.

Does Freecash actually pay?

Freecash provides redemption options for rewards earned through completed activities. Its official information currently lists options including PayPal, bank transfer, cryptocurrency and gift cards, although the exact options available depend on the user's region.

The important distinction is that completing an activity does not automatically guarantee a payout. Freecash's terms say that an offer must be properly completed, tracked and validated before the reward is credited.

This means users should read the requirements of an offer before starting it and keep records of important completion steps when appropriate.

How does Freecash work?

The basic process is simple, although individual offers can have different requirements.

  1. Create or sign in to a Freecash account.
  2. Browse available earning opportunities.
  3. Read the requirements before starting an offer.
  4. Complete the required activity.
  5. Wait for the reward to be credited and validated.
  6. Redeem the available balance through an eligible method.

Freecash's own tutorial describes the process as selecting an offer, completing the task and then cashing out the resulting rewards.

How much can you earn with Freecash?

There is no single amount that every Freecash user will earn. Rewards vary depending on the offer, location, eligibility, completion requirements and available campaigns.

Some offers can advertise substantially larger rewards than ordinary surveys, but higher-paying offers can also require more time, more steps or stricter completion conditions.

Freecash itself describes the platform as a way to earn rewards in spare time rather than as a replacement for a full-time income.

Is there a Freecash bonus?

Freecash currently offers promotional rewards that can include sign-up bonuses and bonus codes. The exact promotion can depend on the user's region and the current campaign.

Freecash's current support information says that eligible new users may receive a $10 account-credit sign-up bonus, although eligibility and promotional conditions can vary.

Freecash also publishes instructions for redeeming bonus codes through its Rewards section. Because promotions change, users should verify the current offer before relying on a specific code or bonus.

What happened with Freecash in 2026?

An important part of evaluating Freecash today is understanding the platform's recent history.

In April 2026, TechCrunch reported that Freecash's mobile apps had been removed from Apple's App Store and Google Play following concerns about misleading marketing and data practices. Almedia, the company behind Freecash, disputed the allegations and said its apps complied with platform policies.

Almedia subsequently announced that Freecash returned to Google Play in May 2026 and to Apple's App Store in June 2026.

This does not mean users should ignore the earlier reporting. It is useful context when evaluating the platform, its advertising and its privacy practices. Readers should consider both the independent reporting and the company's response.

Things to know before using Freecash

  • Some offers have strict completion or tracking requirements.
  • Rewards may require identity or eligibility verification before redemption.
  • The first withdrawal minimum can vary by region.
  • Promotional bonuses and codes can expire or change.
  • Freecash's terms prohibit multiple accounts and certain methods of manipulating or bypassing the platform.
